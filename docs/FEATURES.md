# REVENEX SCHOOL ERP — COMPREHENSIVE FEATURE CATALOG

## 1. Role-Specific Dashboards
- **Principal & Executive Hub**: Live high-level metrics (total enrollment, staff presence, fee collection %, pending leave requests), interactive weekly trend charts, quick admissions, circular publishing.
- **Teacher & Faculty Desk**: Class timetable schedule, one-tap morning roll-call attendance register, homework creator with due dates, study resource uploads.
- **Student Portal**: Today's attendance badge, class schedule, homework tracker with interactive toggle, subject scorecards, digital library access.
- **Parent Self-Service Portal**: Multi-child switcher, real-time child status, fee ledger & 1-tap payment receipt generator, leave applications, class teacher direct dial.

---

## 2. Cross-Role Workflows & Consistency
1. **Attendance Synchronizer**:
   - Marking attendance in Class 10-A instantly updates the class summary, modifies individual student cumulative attendance, and updates parent & student dashboard status badges.
2. **Fee Management & Instant Receipts**:
   - Processing a fee payment decreases the student's outstanding dues, records an immutable transaction with a unique receipt number, updates the collection metrics on the principal dashboard, and notifies the guardian.
3. **Leave Management Cycle**:
   - Parents/Teachers submit leave requests with date ranges and reasons. Principal receives pending badges and can approve or reject with custom remarks. Applicants receive instant status updates.
4. **Academics & Examination**:
   - CBSE/ICSE standard report card generation with subject marks, maximum marks, grade breakdown, class ranking, and principal remarks.
5. **School Operations Suite**:
   - **Live Transport Tracking**: Route stop checkpoints, vehicle tracking status, driver contacts.
   - **Library Management**: Book catalogs, real-time copy availability, loan/return counters.
   - **Campus Inventory**: Physical asset auditing, category filters, working condition tracking.
   - **Notices & Circulars**: Categorized announcements with instant distribution.
   - **AI School Assistant**: Smart natural language assistant for school policy lookups, fee summaries, and syllabus inquiries.
