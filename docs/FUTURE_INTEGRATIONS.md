# REVENEX SCHOOL ERP — FUTURE INTEGRATIONS ROADMAP

## 1. Planned Commercial Integrations
1. **Razorpay & BillDesk Payment Gateway**: Direct integration with UPI, NetBanking, and credit/debit cards for automatic fee reconciliation and instant digital challan generation.
2. **WhatsApp & SMS Gateway**: Automated alerts for absentee warnings, exam dates, fee reminders, and circular links using Twilio / Gupshup.
3. **Biometric & RFID Turnstile Sync**: Automated check-in/check-out logs streamed to Cloud Firestore.
4. **GPS Vehicle Telematics**: Live hardware OBD-II tracker ingestion for real-time bus speeds and geofence alerts.
5. **Gemini AI Intelligent Tutoring**: AI-powered student query assistant and automated assignment grading recommendations for teachers.
